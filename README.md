# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis in a Telecommunications Company

## Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most important business challenges faced by subscription-based companies. Acquiring new customers is often more expensive than retaining existing ones, making customer retention a critical business objective.

This project analyzes customer retention and churn behavior using a telecommunications customer dataset. The objective is to identify the factors associated with customer attrition, understand high-risk customer segments, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer churn and wants to understand:

- Which customers are most likely to leave?
- What factors influence customer churn?
- Which customer segments should be prioritized for retention efforts?
- What business actions can reduce churn and improve customer lifetime value?

---

Dataset: Telco Customer Churn Dataset

## Key Findings

### Overall Churn Rate

- 26.54% of customers churned
- Approximately 1 in every 4 customers left the company

### Highest Risk Segments

| Segment | Churn Rate |
|----------|----------:|
| Month-to-Month + Fiber Optic | 54.61% |
| 0-12 Months Tenure | 47.44% |
| Electronic Check Users | 45.29% |
| Month-to-Month Contract | 42.71% |
| Fiber Optic Internet | 41.89% |

### Service Impact

Customers using the following services showed significantly lower churn rates:

- Online Security
- Tech Support
- Online Backup
- Device Protection

### Revenue Analysis

- Retained Customers Revenue: 13.19 Million
- Churned Customers Revenue: 2.86 Million

---

## High-Risk Customer Persona

The typical high-risk customer profile identified in this analysis is:

- Month-to-Month Contract
- Fiber Optic Internet Service
- Electronic Check Payment Method
- No Online Security
- No Tech Support
- Less Than 12 Months Tenure
- Senior Citizen

Customers exhibiting multiple characteristics from this profile are significantly more likely to churn.

---

## Business Recommendations

### Encourage Long-Term Contracts

Promote One-Year and Two-Year contracts through discounts and loyalty incentives.

### Improve New Customer Retention

Focus retention programs on customers within their first year.

### Investigate Fiber Optic Customer Experience

Analyze pricing, service quality, and customer expectations.

### Promote Security & Support Services

Increase adoption of Online Security and Tech Support offerings.

### Encourage Automatic Payments

Incentivize customers to switch from Electronic Check to automatic payment methods.
